React, Gatsby, TailwindCSS

Chester Court

Website for a landmark Upper West Side cooperative focused on heritage, resident amenities, and a stronger digital presence.

Challenge
The property needed a digital presence that balanced a rich archive of documents and floor plans with an editorial identity — without becoming hard to navigate or feeling like a generic real estate listing.
Approach
Designed and built a Gatsby site with a refined visual hierarchy that leads with heritage storytelling, dedicated sections for amenities, and structured document navigation so floor plans and building materials are easy to find.
Outcome
A premium residential site that gives Chester Court a stronger digital identity and helps prospective residents explore the property at their own pace before making contact.

/03 · Featured
React, Gatsby, TailwindCSS, i18n

Jenny Adventures

Travel agency website with tailored trips across Europe, Mexico, and Canada, built around direct contact, inspiration, and multilingual navigation.

Challenge
The business needed a site that could sell custom travel planning without feeling like a generic agency catalogue. Destinations, offers, and trust cues had to stay clear in English and French while keeping the next step obvious.
Approach
Built a bilingual Gatsby site with destination-led navigation, editorial sections for trip inspiration, and repeated inquiry CTAs tied to direct contact. Structured the pages so the advisor's expertise stayed visible alongside packages and seasonal offers.
Outcome
Created a clearer path from inspiration to inquiry, a stronger specialist positioning, and a site the client can keep evolving as destinations, offers, and travel content change.
Multilingual travel structureDestination-first browsingBooking-focused CTAsPersonal travel specialist positioning
